About

Kaplan Business School (KBS) is part of the Kaplan group, a global education company. It offers business-focused qualifications designed to prepare students for the workplace, with an emphasis on industry relevance and flexible study options.

Areas of study

Business

Foundations in management, marketing, and organisational skills.

Accounting & Finance

Financial reporting, analysis, and professional accounting pathways.

Information Technology

Technology skills applied to modern business problems.

Postgraduate & MBA

Advanced study for professionals building leadership careers.

Key features

  • Industry-focused curriculum
  • Flexible study modes, including online options
  • Support services for students
  • Pathways from short courses through to postgraduate study

Locations

Campuses are located in major Australian cities. Check the official website for current locations and study options.
